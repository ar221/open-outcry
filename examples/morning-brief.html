Receipt required. Preserve source title, public URL, publication date, and the claim each source supports.

PUBLICATION BOUNDARY

Do not publish private notes, local paths, account state, personal workflows, or a frozen status dressed as live evidence.

// 04 · EDITORIAL METHOD

Provenance Method

CAPTURE THE PUBLIC RECORD

Record the source title, publisher, public URL, and publication date. Attach each citation to the sentence it supports rather than leaving evidence in an undifferentiated bibliography.

SEPARATE FACT FROM INTERPRETATION

Label editorial inference, preserve the source's original scope, and exclude private research material from the public receipt. Inspect this specimen's sources.
