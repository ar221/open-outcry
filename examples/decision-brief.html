Decision / published research notes

Freeze the receipt, then re-issue instead of editing.

Write a note’s provenance at publication. Give each fact one owner. When the argument changes, publish a newly dated edition.

Scope
Published notes and reports. Dashboards and the living brand book still resolve current state.
Status
Illustrative decision, not an adopted repository policy. Evidence below comes from public source files.

Consequences

Gain: readers can reconstruct the author’s evidence without a live endpoint. The receipt travels with a checkout, archive or printed copy.

Cost: the facts age. Make the publication date explicit; remove live indicators. Review the receipt with the prose, and retain older editions.

Alternatives

Live lookup
Rejected for dated notes: answers what is true now, not what supported the original argument.
Frozen + live
Rejected: two competing values need explanation and the live half adds an offline failure.
No receipt
Rejected: removes the reader’s direct route to the evidence.
Chosen
Frozen receipt, one owner, a new edition when the argument changes.

Evidence / limits

[01] The brand book now mirrors its version labels from one visible source.
[02] Its token ledger reads the loaded stylesheet: current state is the intended subject.
[03] The contract check asserts hidden-state guards, not provenance accuracy.
