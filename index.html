One identity. Two registers. One tape. The era when markets ran on human voices — open is the machinery, outcry is the voice.

// 01 · THE TWO REGISTERS
BROADCAST — the human voice

Theses, heroes, rhetoric.

CONSOLE — the machine voice

Mechanisms. Data. Warnings.

Green-black CRT. Pixelify display. Amber command ink. Dashboards, terminal output, dense UI.
