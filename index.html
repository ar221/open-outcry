Open Outcry keeps the judgment human and the evidence inspectable.

< act = "V" > rules of the floor

Taste, written down, becomes law. Taste, written down, becomes law.

Each pair states the sanctioned move against the failure it replaces. The governing test never changes: does it feel like a serious operator broadcasting from a live desk?

▸ prohibiteda decorative loop, parallax soup, or bounce easing
plate imagery law

One sourced public-domain engraving family. 1-bit alpha masks, tinted by --r-voice so a single file serves both registers. Native pixel scale, cropped never shrunk. Default .14, hard ceiling .26. aria-hidden, no pointer events, editorial openers only. No named character, no poses, no sticker system, no generated pseudo-history, no unrelated stock art.

promotion doctrine

A lab is evidence, not contract. A primitive enters tokens.css or components.css only when two real consumers independently exhibit the same friction and the new rule reduces total variation. On 2026-08-09 four of the six Lab 06/07 mechanisms cleared that bar and entered the contract: the four-shadow frame, seated fills, selection ink and the plate figure. This page consumes them now like any other surface. Two did not clear it and stay page-local in brandbook.css — the container-relative unit, which Lab 06 rated do not promote on this evidence, and fit-to-measure, which still has one consumer. Being used here is evidence, not entitlement.
